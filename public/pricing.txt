## Booking Terms

- 50% deposit plus a signed contract holds the date
- Remaining 50% due 14 days before the event
- One free date change, to any date within 12 months of the original, subject to availability; the deposit carries over
- Deposit is non-refundable if the client cancels
- The DJ you book is the DJ who plays. In case of illness or emergency, a DJ personally vetted by DJ DX covers the event from the client's planning notes, with a partial refund or credit on the fee
- Written contract on every booking; certificate of insurance (COI) and W-9 available on request
- Full policy: https://djdxmusic.com/booking-policy
